Probabilistic options engine · stdlib core · 0 deps

The edge is the gap between the physical and the risk-neutral distribution.

Forecast the whole distribution of the terminal price (physical P), read the option-implied distribution off the chain (risk-neutral Q), and harvest the variance & skew risk premium in the gap — wired end-to-end through de-Americanization, three regime gates, CVaR risk control, a delta-hedged walk-forward backtest, and an out-of-sample forward-test ledger.

02

What it does

It selects vol structure — is implied variance rich or cheap? — never direction. There is no up/down call anywhere in it, by design.

Read the market (Q)

  • rnd Model-free / BKM variance, skew & kurtosis — forward from put-call parity, spot-anchored.
  • surface SVI fit densifies sparse / truncated chains before extraction.
  • american De-Americanization unlocks QQQ / SPY / single-name chains.

Forecast (P)

  • baseline HAR-RV vol → calm+crash mixture with data-driven skew.
  • mdn / neural Trainable density heads — drop-in, same output type.
  • objective NLL / CRPS / pinball + a decomposed left-tail score.

Decide & gate

  • edge Trades the VRP z-score, not its level — the premium alone is carry, not alpha.
  • strike_scan Per-contract model vs market P(ITM) + EV, BUY/WRITE/FAIR.
  • news + macro Forward-looking vetoes: sentiment, curve, credit, VIX-term, tightening.

Control & prove

  • portfolio Correlation-aware short-vega cap, CVaR sizing, kill-switch.
  • benchmark Signal vs always-sell (income-ETF) vs buy-and-hold, same path.
  • paper_trade Live-forward ledger: the only thing that can prove real edge.

First contact with real data

GOOG daily closes, Aug 2004 – Oct 2008 (1,047 sessions, worst day −12.3%, vol regime ~32% → ~80%). Underlying is real; the option market here is modelled — this tests the machinery on real dynamics, not the 2008 option tape.
